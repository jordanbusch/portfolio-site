ABOUT ME

I'd describe myself as a strong technical thinker with a passion for problem-solving and collaboration in both my professional and personal life. Be it tackling complex coding challenges, finding creative solutions in animation, or playing the right moves in Magic the Gathering, Warhammer or other games, I enjoy the process of analyzing problems and implementing effective solutions.

In 2016 I graduated from NSCCs's digital animation program and began my career as a Builds Artist. For the past 10 years I've developed my skills in 2D rigging and grew into a Lead role at Jam Filled where I managed a team of riggers and contributed to many animated series and a movie. In this role I honed my leadership, collaboration, and technical skills. Now I'm focusing my efforts in web development at NSCC's Full Stack Application Development program. I am looking forward to opportunities to combine my experience in animation with my growing expertise in web development.
